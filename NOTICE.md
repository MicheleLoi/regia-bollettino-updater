## Why this separation

The code is AGPL-3.0 because the **process of building a curated bulletin** is
something MicheleLoi wants to share openly: the architecture, the validation
pipeline, the Haiku prompt template, the schema design are all useful
infrastructure that others should be able to adapt for their own bulletins,
under copyleft terms.

The content is proprietary because the **curatorial work** — choosing which
tasks to scaffold, in which legal areas, with which structural approach, and
maintaining editorial cadence over time — is the value that MicheleLoi
contributes, and that value is meant to support the ongoing work on the
RegIA portfolio (BeccarIA, MHC-L, MHC-H, Recode-IT).

The two licenses do not conflict: someone forking this code (AGPL) and using
it to build a **different** bulletin (with their own curatorial choices, their
own targets, their own published content) is fully entitled to do so under
AGPL. What is reserved is the **specific bulletins published by MicheleLoi**,
not the act of building bulletins in general.
